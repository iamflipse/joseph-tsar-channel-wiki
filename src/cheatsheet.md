## Main points

### 1. Ask what changes after you speak

**Paraphrase:** An executive action question is the filter. The outcome often is the point. He offers 50% of the first idea as enough, and he says 50% is a rough number.

> "What is different in the real world after I finish speaking?"
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

### 2. Hand them the path

**Paraphrase:** A framework cuts the work the listener has to do. DOD is decision, owner, deadline. He also names problem, cause, solution, and PREP: point, reason, example, point.

> "They exist to reduce the cognitive tax that we place on our listener."
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

### 3. Match the amount, then be quiet

**Paraphrase:** Ask how much detail they need, from 1 to 10. That is his route to a higher value per minute. New or odd pairings get a generosity pause. After the point, stop.

> "How much detail do you need on this right now from 1 to 10?"
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

> "It means improving your ability to speak in the clearest, cleanest version of an idea."
>
> [The #1 Habit for Clearer Speaking (Value-Per-Minute)](videos/2026-02-02-The-1-Habit-for-Clearer-Speaking-Value-Per-Minute.md), 2026-02-02.

### 4. Say the destination first

**Paraphrase:** Rambling, in the beacon video, is a path that keeps forking. The beacon is the campfire you name out loud before the fuel.

> "And the beacon is a light, a destination that you can keep your eyes on."
>
> [The “BEACON” Speaking Trick That Stops Rambling](videos/2025-10-25-The-BEACON-Speaking-Trick-That-Stops-Rambling.md), 2025-10-25.

### 5. Close the gap, and do not perform

**Paraphrase:** A large gap between the thought and the sentence reads as uncertainty. Perfectionism, he says, is the demand to perform brilliantly every time you open your mouth. A blank mind can show up on an answer you already had.

> "When the gap between your thinking and your speaking is large, a person's communication starts leaking insecurity."
>
> ["Mental Lag" Is Why You Can't Communicate Well](videos/2026-03-18-Mental-Lag-Is-Why-You-Can-t-Communicate-Well.md), 2026-03-18.

> "All perfectionism is is performance."
>
> [Let’s solve your perfectionism with speaking](videos/2025-09-20-Let-s-solve-your-perfectionism-with-speaking.md), 2025-09-20.

### 6. Collect words by saying them

**Paraphrase:** Remembering a phrase for later is the mistake he names. You try it on. A commonplace book is the list. Finishing the ends of words is his first move against buffer words.

> "Vocabulary is more about muscle than it is memory."
>
> [How I Collect Articulate Language](videos/2025-06-14-How-I-Collect-Articulate-Language.md), 2025-06-14.

## One minute

He is training high performers to walk into a meeting with little prep and still land one action. Decide the change you want in the room. Say that sentence. Use a small frame if the listener needs a path. Ask whether they want a 1 or a 10. Pause when the phrase is new. Stop when the point is done. The rest of the channel is practice around that: words you keep, silence you allow, and a presence he splits into composure, body, authority, and conviction.
