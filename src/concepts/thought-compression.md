# Thought Compression

**The bottleneck is the mouth, not the size of the vocabulary.**

On 31 October 2026 he aims the course at founders, executives, and operators who have to explain something in about 30 seconds with little preparation. He sets aside the usual diagnoses: a better vocabulary, being a rambler, a fast brain, ADHD, more preparation. The picture he uses is a lot of water forced through a small opening.

> "Your brain is processing more information than your words can carry."
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

He names the buildup mental congestion. A founder asked why revenue dropped is holding sales numbers, a new hire, ads, seasonality, the pipeline, customers, an old marketing choice, and a solution, and the mouth can release one word at a time. Wanting to sound intelligent makes the jam worse.

> "And that's why clear speaking is thought compression."
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

> "Compressing what's in our mind into the minimum amount of information that's relevant to our listener."
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

The filter is an executive action question. He says this is the step he has seen change people's speaking the most.

> "An EAQ is what allows you to filter out what is unnecessary."
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

> "What is different in the real world after I finish speaking?"
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

He adds a second question for the person who keeps searching for a perfect answer. He says the first idea is often the relevant one, and that people second-guess it.

> "What is one sentence that captures 50% of the first idea that comes to mind?"
>
> [How To Speak Clearly (Full Course)](videos/2026-10-31-How-To-Speak-Clearly-Full-Course.md), 2026-10-31.

In that same stretch he says 50% is a rough number. It means most of the idea, not every thought in the head.

The long 15 March 2025 course uses a different picture for the same ambition. The people who can think clearly, speak clearly, say how they feel, and be listened to are, in his phrase, verbal athletes.

> "they're what I like to call verbal athletes"
>
> [ARTICULACY: From Monkey To Monk (Full Course)](videos/2025-03-15-ARTICULACY-From-Monkey-To-Monk-Full-Course.md), 2025-03-15.
