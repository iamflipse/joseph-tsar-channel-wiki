# Glossary

His labels, as he uses them in these captions.

- **Mental congestion.** More in the mind than the words can carry. The water and the small opening. [Thought Compression](thought-compression.md).
- **Thought compression.** The minimum that is relevant to the listener. Same course, 31 October 2026.
- **Executive action question (EAQ).** A filter. The one he spells out: what is different in the real world after I finish speaking.
- **Cognitive tax.** The effort a listener spends to understand you. Frameworks exist to reduce it.
- **DOD.** Decision, owner, deadline.
- **PREP.** Point, reason, example, point. Seconds later he also says point, reason, explanation, point.
- **Problem, cause, solution.** The third frame in that course.
- **Overpour.** His word for an answer that is longer than the moment needed. Also "rambler."
- **Zoom.** How much resolution the moment needs. He has people ask for a number from 1 to 10.
- **Value per minute (VPM).** The clearest, cleanest version of the idea. Also, in the later course, the gain from asking how much detail is needed.
- **Generosity pause.** Two uses. A gift of about two or three seconds. And a pause after an uncommon pair of words so the listener can digest them.
- **Mental period.** A period after the sentence, placed in the mind, so the sentence can end. On the clarity course he also says it aloud and taps his fingers.
- **Artificial clock.** Thumb against a finger. Discomfort is the cue to wrap up. His suggestion in that course.
- **Information discipline.** Knowing when to stop. Paired, on 1 June 2026, with a closing bookend.
- **Beacon.** The destination sentence you say first. Also the campfire. Not Reagan's beacon of freedom in the 2023 commentary.
- **Verbal athlete.** His name, in the 15 March 2025 course, for someone who can think clearly, speak clearly, express feeling, and be listened to.
- **The gap.** Space between thinking and speaking. The title says mental lag. The caption says gap.
- **Buffer words.** Likes, ums, you knows, ands, buts, extra conjunctions, used while the thought catches up.
- **Commonplace book.** A running list of phrases. The 17 October 2026 habit.
- **Articulate word.** A word placed on commonness and on precision versus vagueness. 4 May 2023. Not a synonym for an articulate person.
- **Verbal brand.** The boundaries around words and phrases someone will and will not use. 1 October 2024 on this channel, and the separate X-clip wiki.
- **Executive presence.** Composure, physical presence, authority, conviction.
- **Elite Speak.** The program name he uses when he says what "we" teach. Not described here.
