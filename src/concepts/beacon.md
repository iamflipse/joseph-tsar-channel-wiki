# The Beacon

**Name the destination before the path starts to fork.**

The 25 October 2025 video is seven minutes. He draws ordinary speech as a strong start that speeds up, launches into another thought, then another, then a branch off the original, until the speaker is at the mercy of the tongue. A line can also jump to a topic that was not the start.

The fix is not a longer outline. It is a light you can see from inside the tangle.

> "And the beacon is a light, a destination that you can keep your eyes on."
>
> [The “BEACON” Speaking Trick That Stops Rambling](videos/2025-10-25-The-BEACON-Speaking-Trick-That-Stops-Rambling.md), 2025-10-25.

He also calls it the campfire in the middle of the forest. You say it out loud first. The reasons come after. His example answer, about realizing risk was growth, is the beacon sentence, and then he might repeat it. The fuel comes later.

> "So say the beacon out loud first and make a mental note to yourself. This is the campfire."
>
> [The “BEACON” Speaking Trick That Stops Rambling](videos/2025-10-25-The-BEACON-Speaking-Trick-That-Stops-Rambling.md), 2025-10-25.

The word "beacon" shows up in a 2023 commentary video as Reagan's image of freedom. That is the other speaker's figure, not this technique.

This is the close cousin of thought compression. Compression chooses the sentence. The beacon is that sentence said first, so the rest of the talk has somewhere to return. → [Thought Compression](thought-compression.md)
