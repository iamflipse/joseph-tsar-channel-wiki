# Commentary

**31 videos are him watching someone else speak.**

From 2023 he posts reactions and breakdowns: Jobs, JFK, Peterson, Reagan, Churchill, Oppenheimer, Welles, Sinatra, Chaplin, Sagan, Rogers, a news anchor, a TV host, Bobby Kennedy, Robin Williams, Bertrand Russell as captioned "Bertand," Alexander, Nixon's undelivered moon speech, and later MrBeast, Vinh Giang, and a ranking of speakers on the internet. Two Toastmasters tapes of his own are in that group because they are examples on the board, not the later course.

The caption in each of those files is one stream. His comment and the other person's words are not labeled. A line of Churchill in the Churchill file is Churchill. A line about how the speech works is his commentary, when the wording is his. If a sentence could be either, the page leaves it in the caption and does not promote it to a concept.

Three of these had no YouTube English track and were transcribed locally with faster-whisper large-v3-turbo:

- Admiral Picard Delivers POWERFUL Speech On AI (1 August 2023)
- Churchill's "We Shall Fight" Parliament Speech (7 July 2023)
- What Alexander the Great Learned From Aristotle (8 June 2023)

The other commentary files are `youtube-en-orig`.

3 further files are coaching sessions. The titles name the session (control and connection, overthinking, a live articulacy coaching). Turns are not marked.
