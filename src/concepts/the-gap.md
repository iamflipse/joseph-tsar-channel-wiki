# The Gap

**Hesitation, in his account, is space between the thought and the sentence.**

The 18 March 2026 title puts "mental lag" in quotes. The caption does not use that phrase. What he says is a gap.

> "When the gap between your thinking and your speaking is large, a person's communication starts leaking insecurity."
>
> ["Mental Lag" Is Why You Can't Communicate Well](videos/2026-03-18-Mental-Lag-Is-Why-You-Can-t-Communicate-Well.md), 2026-03-18.

He says they sound hesitant and unsure, not because they are weak, but because the mind was given empty space and took it. When the gap is small, thinking and speaking happen together and it feels smooth. Most people, he says, leave too much space between preparing a thought and presenting it.

On 2 May 2023, "How To Speak Like You Think," the older picture is a mind that will not release what you know. He calls the aim a flow where nothing sits between the thought and the sentence. That video's steps are his practices at that date. This page does not restate them as the 2026 gap definition.

The blank-mind video, 8 June 2026, is the meeting version. You knew the answer. Then nothing.

> "Your mind just goes blank."
>
> [Why Your Brain Goes Blank When You Speak (And How to Fix It)](videos/2026-06-08-Why-Your-Brain-Goes-Blank-When-You-Speak-And-How-to-Fix-It.md), 2026-06-08.

He describes hearing yourself start a sentence with no idea how it ends, and filling the quiet with words that are not the point. The worst part, in his telling, is that the answer was there five seconds before the question.

Perfectionism, 20 September 2025, is the demand that every opening of the mouth be a brilliant performance. He uses a character, Johnny, who is trying to solve right-words plus right-context as if it were a math problem that unlocks maximum impact. He says that problem is the fun one because it is basically arithmetic, and then he spends the video on it. The opening line is the claim to keep.

> "All perfectionism is is performance."
>
> [Let’s solve your perfectionism with speaking](videos/2025-09-20-Let-s-solve-your-perfectionism-with-speaking.md), 2025-09-20.

The double "is" is the caption.

On 27 June 2026 he takes writers who are strong on the page and weaker out loud. He says he has worked with many of them, and that he does not have an agenda beyond the patterns he has seen. The gap in that video is between two ways of using words, writing and speaking, not only between a thought and a sentence.

Coaching sits next to these lessons. On 5 June 2026 the title says the client already had the words and his mind was blocking them. On 10 June 2026 the title says overthinking gave way to speaking from the heart. The captions do not mark his voice and the other person's. Read them as sessions, not as a monologue.
