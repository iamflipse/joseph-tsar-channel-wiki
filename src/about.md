# About

This book is 99 long-form videos from the Joseph Tsar channel, https://www.youtube.com/@ttsar/videos, from 10 January 2023 through 14 November 2026. The list is [The 99 Captions](videos.md).

Each page reports what the caption says. A paraphrase on a concept page is marked. A block quote was checked against that video. Titles such as "95% of CEOs" stay titles. Commentary videos mix his remarks with another speaker, and the turns are not labeled. Coaching videos do not say who is speaking.

## How the words got here

96 files are YouTube's English captions (`youtube-en-orig`). Each rolling cue kept its last line. Stutters stay. Three videos had no English track and were transcribed locally with faster-whisper large-v3-turbo: the Churchill reaction (7 July 2023), Alexander and Aristotle (8 June 2023), and the Admiral Picard speech (1 August 2023).

A 10-minute X clip on verbal brand is a different source. [Communicate Like a Leader](https://iamflipse.github.io/joseph-tsar-leader-wiki/) is his November 2026 training, not this channel.
