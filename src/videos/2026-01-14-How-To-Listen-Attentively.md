This is a concept, a graph that's been around for quite some time. What the emotional wheel is is an expansion of the most important emotional vocabulary that you can use to express yourself and to detect emotion in other people. So, the way that this graph is interpreted is at the center we have the six dominant emotions: anger, sadness, surprise, joy, love, and fear. I highly recommend memorizing this circle, or at the bare minimum, just knowing the two inner circles. It helps you articulate with more emotional precision what a person might be feeling.

And what we think about when we're listening to someone is always beginning with first making an emotional guess.
