I came across a fascinating story a few years ago it was a study in fact 5 000 people were asked the question what they considered to be the single most powerful word in the English language the most powerful word among the answers were words like love betrayal God and purpose but there was one story there was one word that was far more common than any other it was the word I gave you a bit of a precursor story the word story was the most common what is a story it's not just a recounting of events a story is yes a recounting of events but more so than that it is a structure it's a way of ordering events and ideas into a narrative that makes sense to us I had finished this book on Saturday one of my favorite books of all time it's called Mythos written by an author by the name of Stephen Fry and this book is simply a compilation of Greek mythology and it made me realize I was awestruck by how common many of the stories written 2 000 years ago are in society today in media in messaging in movies in fact we as humans whether we realize it or not have a profound and deep reverence for story that is just built into our human condition and today I want to take you on a little Adventure as we briefly cover the language of story and I want to give you a framework that can help you use stories to be more impactful in your life it was the 1940s and there was a gentleman by the name of Joseph Campbell and Joseph Campbell gave himself a darn near impossible task he was going to try to read and review every story myth Legend every major story myth and legend in the history of humanity in order to try to find if there was a pattern between the myths of the ancient macedonians the Chinese Legend and lore the text of the Native Americans was there a common pattern between the stories that these cultures produced and passed between generations and it turns out that there was in fact he talked about this in his book the hero's journey or this book was actually called the the hero with a thousand faces and he introduced the concept of the hero's journey he called this the mono myth the core story that singed to surface across every culture and this story template has been referred to as the hero's journey a concept that many of you perhaps have heard in passing and I'm not going to cover all 12 stages of the hero's journey but the core Narrative of this story template is you as the hero the protagonist being taken from your ordinary state of events being met with some kind of hardship facing the Call to Adventure rejecting that call meeting a mentor being thrust into an unknown Realm and meeting adversity hardship succumbing to Temptation and loss facing fear failing facing fear once more and finally vanquishing that fear becoming transformed in the process it's a very detailed cyclical structure that hearing out loud might bring to mind many films that follow this template films like Star Wars The Matrix The Lion King even franchises like Harry Potter are built and use the hero's journey as scaffolding for their World building there are over 200 major films that use the hero's journey framework in fact most notably George Lucas had met with Joseph Campbell and had presented the Star Wars story script that he was working on and said how can we make this one of the most powerful stories in the history of the world and we have the Star Wars plot that perhaps most of you are familiar with today of course not every every story leverages the hero's journey there are many ways to reorder and rearrange these stages to present new meaning but it really does beg the question why are we so fascinated with stories isn't it interesting that every culture seems to fall back on these core components I think it speaks to our Human Condition our Universal desire for purpose and it beautifully illustrates the common imagination that we all have in fact stories are nothing but our attempt to make sense of the world around us there is a very fascinating idea where the story is that attempt to order our events and ideas and in in doing so we take these stories and we apply them to different areas of Our Lives we have to politics has a story that we like to apply to it whether it's one we invented ourselves or one that we absorb from others the events of the world are moving in a certain direction we have a story for our beliefs and there's also the story of our life arguably the most real story to us that story of our life is the focus of us as the protagonist and are we not the protagonists of our own Grand Adventure an adventure that is characterized by periods of richness and liveliness but also met with times of dullness dryness and defeat one of the most impactful lessons I've ever been taught and something I carry with me in my mind to this day is to never compare the troughs of your story to the peaks of someone else's story because our stories are so vastly different and we're all on different Journeys I want to give you a framework a story structure that you can use in your life to enhance the process of Storytelling it's called the perm framework p-e-r-m the P stands for purpose or the P stands for platform the platform event e is for event R is for resolution and M is for meaning platform event resolution and meaning very simply the platform is the ordinary stage of events think of a calm ocean that is almost ripe for disruption the event is the change what happens what what is the Ripple that starts to cause the waves to become tumultuous the resolution is how you deal with that event and finally meaning is how was your outlook and how different in life how have things have changed I'll give you a final example to illustrate all this and wrap up the speech it was Saturday morning this is the platform and I was reading a book on my deck realizing that I had not yet prepared a speech for Toastmasters I completely forgot it was ignorant throughout the entire week and I realized that I had a speech due in two days and I had no material nothing nada zilch I had I I had nothing that was the event the resolution I decided that I would speak on something that I was familiar with in fact I was going to speak on the book that I was reading which was the book of Mythos I mentioned earlier and I spent the morning writing that speech memorizing the speech over the weekend and finally meaning I delivered the speech and in the process was able to speak on something I was learning and Advance my skills as a storyteller and a speaker Mr Toastmaster
