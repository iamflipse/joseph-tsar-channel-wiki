## The argument, as he tells it across the lessons

1. **The mouth is slower than the mind.** On the clarity course he says the usual complaint (vocabulary, rambling, a fast brain) is not the problem. The brain is holding more than the words can carry. He calls that mental congestion, and he calls the skill thought compression. → [Thought Compression](concepts/thought-compression.md)
2. **Find one point, then give the listener a path.** An executive action question filters the flood. Frameworks exist to cut what he calls cognitive tax. He names DOD, problem cause solution, and PREP. → [Frameworks and the Zoom](concepts/frameworks.md)
3. **Stop pouring after the point.** Value per minute is the clearest version of the idea, not a speed. The beacon is a destination you say first so the talk stops forking. A generosity pause and a mental period are how he tells people to leave silence and to finish. → [Value per Minute](concepts/value-per-minute.md) · [The Beacon](concepts/beacon.md)
4. **A gap between thinking and speaking shows up as hesitation.** The video titled mental lag never uses those two words as a phrase. He talks about the gap. The blank-mind video and the perfectionism video are the same neighborhood: you knew it, then the performance took over. Writers, he says, can have a separate gap between the page and the voice. → [The Gap](concepts/the-gap.md)
5. **Words are a muscle, and a boundary.** A commonplace book, trying a phrase on, and finishing the ends of words are the practices. In 2023 he draws an articulate word on two axes, commonness and precision. In 2024 he defines a verbal brand as the words someone will and will not use. That short X clip has its own wiki. → [Words](concepts/words.md)
6. **Presence is four qualities he says you can train.** Composure, physical presence, authority, conviction. Assertiveness, in the course, is not proof someone else is weak. Listening fails, he says, when you catch the fact and miss the emotion. → [Presence](concepts/presence.md)

## What these pages do not do

- They do not turn a commentary video into his own speech. Churchill, Jobs, Reagan, and the rest remain the other speaker. His remarks sit in the same caption. → [Commentary](concepts/commentary.md)
- They do not treat a title as a finding. "95% of CEOs", "300%", and "dangerously articulate" are titles. The claim to keep is the one in the caption.
- They do not merge two years into one definition when the wording changed. The mental period is quoted from the video that defines it.
- They do not label turns in the coaching captions. Those three files are included whole.
- Elite Speak is the program he names. This wiki does not describe the offer.
